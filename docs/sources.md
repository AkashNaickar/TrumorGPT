## Removed / not used (could not verify)

- "1 in 4 AI answers are hallucinated" - no primary source located; dropped from the deck.
- "800+ lives lost in 3 months" - a figure from the student's earlier deck that we could not
  trace; dropped.
- "600+ AI sites" - replaced by the verified NewsGuard figure (3,749 AI content-farm sites).
